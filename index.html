2026 Strategic Priorities
Group-wide themes that run through every brand plan
PRIORITY 01
EV Transition Leadership
Drive electric adoption across BYD, CUPRA, VW, Peugeot and the new OMODA/JAECOO range. Education-first campaigns, home charger incentives, BIK savings calculators for fleet. Target conquest of Tesla and BMW i-series owners.
PRIORITY 02
Digital Lead Generation
Group-level PPC, AutoTrader prominence, social-first content strategy led by group social team. Target 2,400+ digital leads/month across all brands. Sub-£38 cost-per-lead across premium brands, sub-£22 mainstream.
PRIORITY 03
Fleet & B2B Growth
Leverage Swansway's Fleet Procure Dealer Group of the Year win. Cross-brand fleet proposition — VW Commercial, Audi, Land Rover and Peugeot fleet packages. LinkedIn B2B outreach, Chamber of Commerce partnerships, fleet drive days at Crewe HQ.
PRIORITY 04
Aftersales Revenue
Grow service retention to 70%+ across all brands via lifecycle CRM, service plan promotions and MOT reminder campaigns. Target lapsed customers 3+ years. Bodyshop at Crewe as additional revenue stream for all brands.
PRIORITY 05
Community & Family Values
Amplify the Swansway family story — Smyth family 55+ years. Partnerships with Cheshire, Staffordshire and Lancashire community foundations. Crewe Alexandra, CUPRA padel tennis. Local sponsorships as brand-building at each site.
PRIORITY 06
Approved Used at Scale
Motor Match 5 sites plus approved used at all 30 franchised dealerships. Daily stock pricing discipline, AutoTrader Top Value Dealer status maintenance. Finance-first messaging. Target conquest from CarGurus and Autotrader supermarkets.
